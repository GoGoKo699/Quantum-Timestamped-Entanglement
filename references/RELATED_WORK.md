# Related work and attribution

| Work | Established ingredient and relation to this problem |
| --- | --- |
| [Kalb et al., *Science* 356, 928–932 (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan0070) | Physical one-photon heralding, local storage and two-copy distillation at solid-state nodes. The present receiver builds on this link architecture. |
| [Rozpędek et al., *Phys. Rev. A* 97, 062333 (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.97.062333), Sec. IV.D and Appendix H | Two-input EPL distillation, including the correlated unknown phase, phase drift and one false-click product component. The paper gives the optimal dyad output and its distillable entanglement. The dyad yield in the Poisson-stream calculation is inherited. |
| [Chi, Kim and Lee, *Phys. Lett. A* 376, 143–146 (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physleta.2011.11.006) and [Dirnegger et al., *Quantum Sci. Technol.* 11, 015026 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1088/2058-9565/ae2884) | Three-to-one distillation and transducer-focused simulations that compare it with EPL. Dirnegger et al. model both false-click products and memory dephasing. Their three-to-one benchmark uses bilateral controlled-NOT gates and postselection. The receiver here uses a local total-count measurement, a qutrit filter, and a timestamp-selected fallback branch. Its comparison is an analytical success-versus-fidelity bound within the specified correlated stream. |
| [Zeng et al., *Nat. Commun.* 13, 3903 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-31534-7) and [Lu et al., *Phys. Rev. Research* 7, 023102 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.7.023102) | Flexible short-gap mode pairing, its rate crossover, and an entanglement-based free-pairing model for key distribution. These works already give the pairing mechanism and performance improvements in their own task. The ideal stream here counts distilled ebits and establishes a sharp asymptotic rate against arbitrary collective LOCC in its Poisson and Brownian source. |
| [Wiseman and Vaccaro, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 91, 097902 (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.91.097902) | Activation of usable entanglement in fixed local particle-number sectors. Local number projection is an established principle. The timing-dependent gains here arise from the finite decoder, fidelity-matched two-input comparison and the stochastic stream analysis. |
| [Devetak and Winter, *Proc. R. Soc. A* 461, 207–235 (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2004.1372) | One-way hashing achievability of coherent information. The maximally correlated states in the present source have a matching diagonal relative-entropy comparison. |

The Poisson-stream converse applies to raw heralds with the stipulated independent arrival and wrapped phase law. Neither it nor the finite examples infer a rate-versus-fidelity curve for a particular experimental platform.
